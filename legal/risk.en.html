2. Delayed and limited data

The market data shown by the application is delayed by at least 15–16 minutes and covers a limited number of United States equities. It is not real-time quotes. The Broker / Trader module simulates orders in a local practice portfolio or in an Alpaca paper (test) account. It does not move real money. Real orders cannot be sent from the interface. Simulations ignore or approximate commissions, taxes, liquidity, slippage and real execution; their results are not reproducible in a real market.

The application expressly flags when evidence is insufficient; even when it does not, no pattern has demonstrated predictive value. The prediction experiment included in the application did not beat a basic baseline and is shown for transparency only.
