Privacy Policy — Signal Observatory

Version 1.0 · Last updated: 27 October 2026 (Última actualización: 27 de octubre de 2026)

This Policy explains what personal data Sebastián Azzam, a natural person domiciled in Chile (the "Controller" or "we"), processes in connection with the Signal Observatory program (the "Software"), the store signalobservatory.lemonsqueezy.com, Mercado Pago payments, email support and the Discord community.

Core idea: the Software runs on your own computer. It has no user account on our servers, sends no telemetry or usage statistics, and we do not see your market data, notes, simulated portfolios or credentials. The only personal data that reaches us is what you give us when you buy, request a key, write to us or verify on Discord.

We comply with Chile's Law No. 19,628 on the protection of private life and Law No. 21,719 (which enters into full force on 1 December 2026 and whose standards we already apply) and, where applicable, the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the UK GDPR, Brazil's LGPD and U.S. state privacy laws.

7. Security

  • License keys are verified with elliptic-curve cryptography; the issuing private key is stored encrypted and offline.
  • Local credentials and secrets use Windows DPAPI, bound to your account.
  • The embedded database listens only on the local interface and requires SCRAM authentication.
  • The local service refuses writes coming from other websites (origin check).
  • Every release includes a signed integrity manifest; a modified copy refuses to run.
  • The issuer's key ledger is kept on the Controller's computer, encrypted by the operating system, with an offline backup.

No system is infallible. If we become aware of a breach affecting your data, we will notify you, and the competent authority, within the legal time limits (Law 21,719: without undue delay; GDPR: 72 hours to the authority).

How to exercise them: write to sebastianazzam34@gmail.com from the email used for the purchase, or stating your order number or license identifier. Complaints: you may complain to Chile's Agencia de Protección de Datos Personales (once operational under Law 21,719) or, in the meantime, to the Chilean courts under Law 19,628; if you reside in the EU/EEA, to your country's supervisory authority; in the United Kingdom, to the ICO; in Brazil, to the ANPD. California and other U.S. state residents: you have the right to know, delete and correct your data and not to be discriminated against for exercising those rights. We do not sell or share personal information. You may exercise your rights at the same email.

Local data: because that data is only on your computer, you control it directly: you can delete it with the uninstaller or by removing the %LOCALAPPDATA%\SignalObservatory folder. To delete your Discord link, use the bot command or write to us.

The Software and the community are intended for persons aged 18 or over. We do not knowingly collect data from minors. If you believe a minor has given us data, write to us and we will delete it.

10. Discord: verification details

When you join the server, a bot asks for your license key or identifier (or a one-time code we send you). The bot checks the license's signature and validity, assigns the corresponding role (Basic / Pro) and stores the link Discord ID ↔ license ID, the date and the plan. That link is used to (a) remove the role when the license expires; (b) prevent one license from verifying several accounts; and (c) apply the sanctions ladder in the Community Rules, including, in serious cases, revocation. The bot does not read your private messages or those on other servers. Messages you post on our server are subject to Discord's policy and visible to other members.
